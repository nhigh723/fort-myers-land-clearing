How it works

  1. Describe the lot

    Size, what's growing on it, and what you plan to do with it. The more detail, the more accurate the price.

  2. Local pros review it

    Your request goes to land-clearing professionals who work in Fort Myers. They may ask for photos or a site visit.

  3. Compare and decide

    You get pricing directly from the pros — usually within one business day. No obligation, no pressure.

What to include for an accurate price

Three things drive your price more than anything: how thick the growth is, whether stumps come out or get ground, and whether debris is hauled off or mulched in place. Mention all three in the job description — and say whether the lot is near water or has storm debris — and your quotes will be far more accurate.
